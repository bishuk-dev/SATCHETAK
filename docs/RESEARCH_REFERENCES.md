## Key product facts reflected in scope

- Sentinel-2 is designed for vegetation/agriculture and land-cover monitoring.
- Its common high-resolution bands are 10 m, so the product must remain resolution-aware.
- Optical monitoring is periodic and may be limited by clouds; do not market it as guaranteed real-time surveillance.
